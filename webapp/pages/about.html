Why a squire?

In the old stories, a squire stood beside a knight: carrying the gear, keeping things in good order, and learning the craft along the way. That's my job here. AI assistants are powerful helpers, but someone has to show you how to get them working for you. I'm Orin, and I'll be that someone.

Who this site is for

Agent Squire is for people who want AI to help with their work and life, not for people who build AI. If you've tried ChatGPT or Copilot and wondered "is this all it does?", or you've been meaning to try one and don't know where to start, you're in the right place. No coding needed.

How I help

  • Plain language. When a term like "agent" or "MCP" comes up, I explain it the first time I use it.
  • Real tasks. Guides start from something you want done, not from a feature list.
  • Honest advice. If an assistant isn't the right tool for a job, I'll say so.
  • Dated guides. AI tools change quickly, so every guide says when it was last checked.
  • Your data stays yours. The tools on this site run in your browser, so what you type isn't sent anywhere.

Who's behind Orin

Agent Squire is written by Erik Gassler, a software engineer with almost twenty years of experience, who works alongside AI agents every day. It's part of Stoic Dreams, Erik's software studio.
