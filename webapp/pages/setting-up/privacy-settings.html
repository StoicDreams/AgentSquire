A few minutes in your assistant's settings can make a big difference to what it keeps about you. Here's what to look for, where to find it in the big four, and what to do if you've already shared something you'd rather not have.

What to look for

Every assistant has slightly different settings, but these are the ones that matter most.

  • Training on your chats: whether your conversations can be used to improve future versions. Many assistants leave this on unless you turn it off.
  • Chat history: how long your conversations are kept, and how to delete them.
  • Temporary chats: a private mode for one-off questions that aren't saved to your history or memory. It may be called temporary, incognito, or private.
  • Memory: what it has saved about you. See Memory and projects.
  • Connected apps: which of your accounts it can reach. See Connecting your apps.
  • Shared links: any chats you've shared with a link, which anyone with the link can see.

Turning off training usually only affects future chats. It doesn't delete what's already there.

Where to find them

These were checked in November 2026. Menus change, so if you can't find a setting, look under Settings for Privacy, Data, or Personalization.

  • ChatGPT: open Settings, then Data Controls, and look for "Improve the model for everyone". Temporary Chat is in the chat menu.
  • Claude: open Settings, then Privacy, and look for the setting about helping improve Claude. Incognito chats are available from a new chat.
  • Gemini: look for Gemini Apps Activity in your Google account or Gemini's settings. Turning off "Keep activity" stops chats being saved there, and gives you the option to delete past activity.
  • Microsoft Copilot: look in Settings under Privacy. Copilot's options have changed between versions, so what you see may differ. Check what it says about training, history, and memory.

If you use an assistant through work or school, your organization controls many of these settings, and work accounts are often not used for training. Ask your IT team if you're unsure.

Good habits

  • Decide on training once. Turn it off if you'd rather your chats weren't used, and check again after big app updates.
  • Use temporary chats for sensitive questions, like health or money worries.
  • Clean up now and then. Delete old chats you don't need, and review saved memories.
  • Remove shared links you no longer need.
  • Remember that settings aren't everything. Even with training off, companies may keep chats for a while for safety and legal reasons. The best protection is not sharing what you'd mind losing. See What not to share.

If you've already shared something

  1. Delete the chat, and remove any shared link to it.
  2. Check memory and delete anything it saved from that chat.
  3. Change any password or code you shared, and contact your bank if you shared financial details.
  4. Turn off training if you haven't already, so future chats aren't used.
