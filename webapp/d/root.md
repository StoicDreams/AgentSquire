## Hello, I'm Orin, your squire

AI assistants like ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Copilot can do much more than answer questions. They can sort your inbox, summarize a meeting, plan a trip, or tidy up a spreadsheet. Getting them there shouldn't take a computer science degree.

I'm here to help you choose the right assistant, set it up, and put it to work, in plain language and one step at a time.
