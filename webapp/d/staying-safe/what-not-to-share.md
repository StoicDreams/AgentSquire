<webui-quote theme="title">
Talking to an AI assistant feels private, like thinking out loud. It isn't. What you type goes to the company that runs it, where it may be stored, read, or used for training. Here's what to keep out, and how to still get help with sensitive things.
</webui-quote>

## Never share

<webui-page-segment elevation="10">

- **Passwords, PINs, and security codes,** including one-time codes sent to your phone.
- **Card, bank, and account numbers.**
- **Government ID numbers,** like a Social Security number, passport, or driver's license number.
- **Answers to security questions,** like your mother's maiden name or first pet.
- **Login details for other people,** or anything that would let someone into an account.

No legitimate assistant needs any of these to help you. If an assistant, app, or website that claims to be one asks for them, treat it as a scam.

</webui-page-segment>

## Think twice before sharing

<webui-page-segment elevation="10">

- **Health details,** yours or anyone else's.
- **Money details,** like your income, debts, or exact balances.
- **Legal matters,** like a dispute, a divorce, or anything involving the police.
- **Other people's information,** like names, addresses, phone numbers, and photos of friends, family, or customers.
- **Work information,** like customer data, contracts, plans, or anything marked confidential. Many workplaces don't allow it in personal AI accounts.
- **Your exact location and daily routine,** like your home address and when you're away.
- **Photos and documents** with private details in them, like a bill showing your account number or a screenshot of your messages.

</webui-page-segment>

## How to get help anyway

<webui-page-segment elevation="10">

You can still get help with private matters by leaving out what identifies you.

- **Swap in made-up details.** "My friend Alex" instead of a real name, or "about $50,000" instead of an exact figure.
- **Describe instead of pasting.** Explain what a letter says instead of uploading the letter with your account number on it.
- **Cover or crop.** Black out names, numbers, and addresses in screenshots and documents before you upload them.
- **Ask in general terms.** "What are common reasons a tax refund is delayed?" works without your tax details.
- **Use a temporary chat** for sensitive questions, so they're not saved to your history or memory.
- **Check your settings.** Turn off training on your chats if you prefer. See [Privacy settings](/setting-up/privacy-settings).

</webui-page-segment>

## A quick test

<webui-page-segment elevation="10">

Before you press send, ask yourself: **would I be comfortable if a stranger at the company read this?** If not, take out the private parts, or don't send it.

</webui-page-segment>

## If you've already shared something

<webui-page-segment elevation="10">

Delete the chat and any shared link to it, remove anything saved to memory, and change any password or code you shared. If you shared financial details, contact your bank. [Privacy settings](/setting-up/privacy-settings) has the steps.

</webui-page-segment>
