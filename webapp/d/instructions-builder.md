## What are custom instructions?

<webui-page-segment elevation="10">

Every time you start a new chat, your assistant knows nothing about you. Custom instructions fix that. They're a short note, saved in your assistant's settings, that it reads before every conversation. Tell it who you are and how you like answers once, and you don't have to repeat yourself.

Each assistant names this a little differently, such as custom instructions, personal preferences, or instructions for Gemini, but they all work the same way.

</webui-page-segment>

## How to use the builder

<webui-page-segment elevation="10">

1. **Answer what you like.** Every question is optional. Even one or two answers make a difference.
1. **Pick your assistant.** I'll show you where its instructions live.
1. **Copy and paste.** Use the copy button on your instructions, then paste them into your assistant's settings and save.
1. **Try it.** Start a new chat and ask something you often ask. If the answer still isn't right, come back, change an answer, and paste again.

</webui-page-segment>

## Tips from your squire

<webui-page-segment elevation="10">

- **Start small.** A few clear lines work better than a long list. You can always add more later.
- **Be specific.** "I'm a teacher" helps. "I teach fifth grade science" helps more.
- **Say what bothers you.** If answers are always too long or full of jargon, say so. That's often the biggest improvement.
- **Leave out private details.** Don't put passwords, account numbers, or anything you wouldn't want stored in your instructions. They're saved with your account.
- **Check it now and then.** As you use your assistant for new things, update your instructions to match.

</webui-page-segment>

## An example

<webui-page-segment elevation="10">

Here's what someone who runs a small bakery might end up with:

```text:Example instructions
About me:
- Please call me Sam.
- I work as the owner of a small bakery.
- I mostly use you for writing and replying to email, and planning projects, trips, and events.

How to respond:
- Be warm and friendly, like a helpful colleague.
- Keep answers short. Give the answer first, and only add detail if I ask.
- Use plain language, and explain any technical term the first time you use it.
- If my request is unclear, ask me a question before answering.
```
