## Three ways to give your assistant context

<webui-page-segment elevation="10">

| Feature | What it is | Best for |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Custom instructions | A short note about you and how you like answers, read before every chat | Who you are, your tone, and habits you always want |
| Memory | Details the assistant saves from your chats and recalls later | Facts that come up naturally, like your job or your kids' ages |
| Projects | A space that groups related chats with their own files and instructions | Ongoing work, like a house move, a class, or a client |

Each assistant names these a little differently. Look in its settings and sidebar for words like Personalization, Memory, Instructions, Projects, Gems, or Notebooks. Not every plan includes every feature, and they change often.

</webui-page-segment>

## Custom instructions

<webui-page-segment elevation="10">

Custom instructions are the simplest and most predictable of the three, because you write them yourself and they don't change unless you change them. Start here.

The [Instructions Builder](/instructions-builder) asks a few questions, writes them for you, and shows you where to paste them in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Copilot.

</webui-page-segment>

## Memory

<webui-page-segment elevation="10">

With memory turned on, an assistant can save details from your conversations, like "has two teenagers" or "is training for a half marathon", and use them in later chats without being told again. Some assistants also look back over your past chats for context.

It's handy, but it means the assistant keeps more about you, and it can remember things wrongly or bring up an old detail where it doesn't belong.

**Keep it under control**

- **See what it saved.** Your assistant's settings usually show a list of saved memories. Read it now and then.
- **Delete what's wrong or private.** Remove anything out of date, mistaken, or that you'd rather it didn't keep.
- **Tell it directly.** You can usually say "remember that I'm vegetarian" or "forget that I mentioned my health condition".
- **Use a temporary chat for sensitive questions.** Most assistants have a temporary, incognito, or private chat that isn't saved to memory or your history.
- **Turn it off** if you'd rather each chat started clean. Custom instructions still work without memory.

</webui-page-segment>

## Projects

<webui-page-segment elevation="10">

A project is a folder for one piece of ongoing work. Chats inside it share the same files and instructions, and stay separate from your other chats. Good uses include:

- **Planning a move,** with your checklist, budget, and the new home's details.
- **A class or course,** with the syllabus and your notes.
- **A small business task,** like your price list and the tone you use with customers.
- **A long project at home,** like a garden plan or a family history.

**How to set one up**

1. Create a new project and give it a clear name.
1. Add instructions for that project only, like "This project is about planning our move to Denver in June. Keep answers practical and budget-minded."
1. Add the files it should know about. Remove personal details you don't need it to have first.
1. Start your chats inside the project, so they all share the same background.

</webui-page-segment>

## Which should I use?

<webui-page-segment elevation="10">

- **Start with custom instructions.** They give the biggest improvement for the least effort.
- **Add a project** when you find yourself pasting the same files or background into several chats.
- **Leave memory on or off** depending on how you feel about the assistant keeping details about you. Either way, check what it has saved.

For more on what assistants keep and how to limit it, see [Privacy settings](/setting-up/privacy-settings).
