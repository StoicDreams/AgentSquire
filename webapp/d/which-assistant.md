## How I decide

<webui-page-segment elevation="10">

The four big assistants can all write, answer questions, search the web, and read the files you give them. For most people, the best fit comes down to a few things:

1. **What your work or school allows.** If they give you an assistant, use that one for work. It's the one they've approved for work information.
1. **The apps you already use.** Gemini works inside Gmail and Google Docs. Copilot works inside Word, Outlook, and Excel with Microsoft 365. An assistant inside the apps you use every day saves a lot of copying and pasting.
1. **What you'll do most.** Some assistants make pictures and some don't. Some are known for their writing. These differences matter more than which one scores best on a test.

</webui-page-segment>

## The four at a glance

<webui-page-segment elevation="10">

- **ChatGPT:** the most widely used assistant, with lots of guides and help available. It can write, search the web, and make pictures.
- **Claude:** known for clear, natural writing and for working through long documents. It can draw charts and diagrams, but doesn't make pictures or photos.
- **Gemini:** Google's assistant. It works inside Gmail, Docs, and other Google apps, and can make pictures.
- **Microsoft Copilot:** Microsoft's assistant. It works inside Word, Outlook, Excel, and other Microsoft apps when you have Microsoft 365, and can make pictures.

All four have a free plan with limits. Their main paid plans cost about $20 a month in the US. Prices and plans change often, so check each assistant's own pricing page before you pay.

</webui-page-segment>

## Try before you pay

<webui-page-segment elevation="10">

- **Test with real work.** Give your top two the same task you actually need done, like a reply you've been putting off. You'll learn more than from any comparison.
- **Pay attention to how it feels.** You'll use your assistant a lot, so pick the one whose answers you find easiest to work with.
- **Start on the free plan.** Upgrade only when you keep hitting the free plan's limits.
- **Set it up once you've chosen.** The [Instructions Builder](/instructions-builder) tells your assistant about you, so its answers fit you from the start.

</webui-page-segment>
