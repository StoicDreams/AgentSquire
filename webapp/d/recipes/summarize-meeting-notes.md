Meeting notes are only useful if someone reads them. Give your assistant your notes, and it will pull out what was decided, who is doing what, and what is still open, in a summary people will actually read.
</webui-quote>

## What you'll need

<webui-page-segment elevation="10">

- Your notes from the meeting, or a transcript if your meeting app made one. Rough notes are fine.
- What the meeting was about, in a few words.
- Who will read the summary, such as your team, your manager, or a client.

</webui-page-segment>

## How to use it

<webui-page-segment elevation="10">

1. **Fill in the blanks** and paste your notes into the last box.
1. **Copy the prompt** and paste it into a new chat with your assistant.
1. **Check the action items.** Make sure each one has the right owner. Anything marked "not stated" needs a name or a date from you.
1. **Ask for changes,** such as "shorter", "as an email to the team", or "only the action items".
1. **Share it yourself,** after reading it through.

</webui-page-segment>

## Tips from your squire

<webui-page-segment elevation="10">

- **Write notes soon after.** Even a few lines written right after the meeting give a better summary than a perfect memory a week later.
- **Note names and dates.** The assistant can only list an owner or a deadline that's in your notes. The prompt tells it not to guess, so "Maria will send the budget by Friday" is worth writing down.
- **Ask before recording.** If you use a transcript, make sure everyone in the meeting knew it was being recorded.
- **Keep confidential meetings out.** Check your workplace's rules before pasting notes about people, pay, health, or clients into an AI assistant.

</webui-page-segment>

## An example

<webui-page-segment elevation="10">

After an hour-long meeting about a spring fundraiser, a volunteer pastes half a page of notes. The summary that comes back has a three-sentence overview, two decisions (the date and the venue), four action items with names, and one open question: who is handling ticket sales. That question goes straight into the email to the group.
